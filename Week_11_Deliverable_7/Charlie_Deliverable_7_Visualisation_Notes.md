I ran the updated Airbnb pipeline with the July and August data and looked at the five graphs it produced. These are the main things I noticed:

1. **July vs August prices:** The two months were really similar. The median nightly price was about **$225 in July** and **$223 in August**.

2. **Types of accommodation:** Most of the observations were **entire homes or apartments** (19,186), followed by **private rooms** (4,841). There were hardly any hotel or shared rooms.

3. **Prices by room type:** Private and shared rooms were generally cheaper. Entire homes and hotel rooms cost more, and prices for entire homes varied quite a bit.

4. **Where the listings were:** **Central Ward** had by far the most Airbnb observations, with **Banks Peninsula** next. The listings weren't spread evenly across Christchurch.

5. **Prices over time:** The median nightly price went from about **$160 in October 2025** to **$235 in January 2026**, then **$217 in April** and **$224 in the July quarter**.

**A couple of things to keep in mind:** The same property can appear in more than one month's data, so the counts aren't necessarily unique Airbnbs. The July-quarter figure uses July and August data. The graphs leave out prices above $1,000, and this report looks at Airbnb rather than long-term rentals.
